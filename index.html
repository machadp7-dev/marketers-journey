The marketer’s road: past, present and future

In the past, marketers wanted reviews and approvals to be faster and to own their content. Today they have both. What came with it is a more complex process and more roles on one desk, and outcomes not yet at the standard marketers want. Closing that gap is what the future is for. This is the measured story, in their words and in the numbers, from February 2025 to the 2027 plan.
